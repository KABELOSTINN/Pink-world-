Our Story

pink world was born in 2024 in johannesburg by creative Kabelo Austin Moima also known as Stinn. The goal is to make boys and girls to feel more comfortable in wearing pink clothing pieces after the belief that pink is actually for girls,and boys can also get to feel cute and confident while wearing the pink clothing. letting go of the boring clothes and creating clothing that celebrate personlity and self expression.

From casual everyday wear to statement pieces,every item is designed for fashion consious young adults that are not afraid to break rules and barries in order for thewm to feel like themselves.
